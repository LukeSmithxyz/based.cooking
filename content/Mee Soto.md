Mee soto is a spicy noodle soup dish that features thick yellow Hokkien noodles served in a flavorful chicken broth known as soto ayam. The broth is enriched with a blend of spices such as ginger, garlic, and lemongrass, and is typically topped with shredded chicken, boiled bean sprouts, and garnished with spring onions, parsley and fried onions.

- ⏲️ Prep time: 20 min
- 🍳 Cook time: 45 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 6

## Ingredients (Bahan)

| Quantity (Kuantiti) | English | Bahasa Melayu |
| 1 whole | chicken about 1.2kg (divide into 4) | ayam dalam kira-kira 1.2kg (bagikan kepada 4) |
| 4 | onions | bawang besar |
| 6 cloves | garlic | bawang putih |
| 1 inch | ginger | halia |
| 1 inch | galangal | lengkuas |
| 8 | candle-nuts (optional) | kacang lilin |
| 3 | lemongrass slightly crushed | serai |
| 5 | pandan leaves | daun pandan |
| 4 tablespoon | Soto or soup mix | rempah soto |
| 1 stick | cinnamon | kayu manis |
| 2 | chicken cube (optional) | kiub ayam |
| 4 L | water | air |
| 1 kg | yellow noodles/vermicelli | mee kuning/mee hoon |
| 200 g | bean-sprouts | tauge |
| 1/2 cup | spring onion | bawang daun |
| 1/2 cup | chopped parsley | daun parsley cincang |
| 1 tub | Fried Shallots | bawang goreng |

## Methods (Caranya/Peraturannya)


For the chili condiment (Untuk perasa cili)

- Blend all of the ingredients till fine. (Kisar semua bahan sehingga halus.)


For the soup (Untuk Sup)

1. Blend onion, garlic, ginger, galangal and candlenuts till a fine paste.
    (Kisar bawang, bawang putih, halia, lengkuas dan kacang lilin sehingga menjadi pes halus.)

2. Add some oil in a pot and fry the blended ingredients with lemongrass and cinnamon.
    (Tambah sedikit minyak dalam periuk dan goreng bahan yang telah dicampur bersama serai dan kayu manis.)

3. Add in the soto/soup mix and cook this for about 5 minutes.
    (Tambah rempah soto dan masak selama kira-kira 5 minit.)

4. Add the chicken and continue cooking for another 5 minutes.
    (Masukkan ayam dan teruskan memasak selama 5 minit lagi.)

5. Pour in the 4L water and add the pandan leaves and chicken cube.
    (Tuangkan air 4L dan masukkan daun pandan serta kiub ayam.)

6. Season with salt and cook for about 20-30 minutes till the chicken is very tender.
    (Perasakan dengan garam dan masak selama kira-kira 20-30 minit sehingga ayam sangat lembut.)

7. Remove chicken from the soup using a pair of tongs. Tear chicken into strips. Set it aside.
    (Keluarkan ayam dari sup menggunakan penyepit. Koyak kan ayam menjadi jalur. Ketepikan.)

8. You can either strain the soup or just use a ladle to remove the lemongrass, pandan leaves and cinnamon.
    (Anda boleh menapis sup atau menggunakan senduk untuk mengeluarkan serai, daun pandan dan kayu manis.)


To serve (Hias dengan daun bawang, daun daun, bawang goreng, dengan perasa cili di sisi.)

1. In a small pot of boiling water, boiled the yellow noodles/vermicelli till it softens. Strain and place in a bowl.
    (Di dalam periuk kecil yang berisi air mendidih, rebus mee kuning/mee hoon sehingga . Tapis dan letakkan di dalam mangkuk.)

2. Boiled the bean-sprouts for 15 seconds, drain and top it on the noodles.
    (Rebus tauge selama 15 saat, tos dan letakkan di atas mee.)

3. Top noodles with the soup and chicken strips.
    (Taburkan mee dengan sup dan jalur ayam.)

4. Garnish with spring onion, parsley, fried onions, with the chili condiment by the side.
    (Hias dengan daun bawang, daun parsli, bawang goreng, dengan perasa cili di sisi.)
