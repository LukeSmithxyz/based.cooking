- ⏲️ Prep time: 10 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- 4 large tomatoes
- 1 pound mozzarella
- 1 bunch basil leaves
- Olive oil
- Balsamic vinegar

## Directions

1. **Prepare Tomatoes and Mozzarella:** Slice tomatoes and mozzarella into equal, 1/4 inch slices.
2. **Arrange Salad:** Place slices of tomato, mozarella, and basil leafs in alternating order on a plate
3. **Season:** Drizzle the salad with olive oil and balsamic vinegar. Season with salt and black pepper.
4. **Serve:** Serve at room temperature.
